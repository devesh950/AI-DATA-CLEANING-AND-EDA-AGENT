The Core Problem

Your data isn't the problem.
Knowing what to do with it is.

Businesses already have plenty of numbers. The challenge is spending hours deciphering spreadsheets instead of making critical decisions.

📊

Data is scattered and uncleaned

CSV exports from billing, payment gateways, and ad channels come riddled with missing values, duplicate rows, and inconsistent date formats.

⏳

Reports take hours to assemble

Finance teams and operators spend entire days manually building pivot tables, writing VLOOKUPs, and assembling charts for weekly meetings.

Important changes are discovered late

Margin erosion, churn concentration, and abnormal expense spikes often go unnoticed until monthly or quarterly financials are closed.

📉

Spreadsheets don't explain why

Raw charts show that a number dropped, but they don't explain what caused the shift or which customer cohort drove the variance.

👥

No dedicated data analyst

Hiring a full-time business analyst costs $100k+/year and requires complex data warehousing. Growing teams need immediate answers today.

🎯

Action ambiguity

Executives don't need 50 charts. They need a concise brief stating what changed, what needs attention, and what action to prioritize next.

Security & Confidentiality

Built for Sensitive Business Data

We know business numbers are strictly confidential. Here is exactly how Metriva safeguards your data.

🔒

In-Memory Processing

Your uploaded datasets are parsed and evaluated in temporary volatile memory during your active session. Raw records are never permanently persisted to disk or databases.

🛡️

Zero Model Training

Customer transaction rows, billable rates, and margin metrics are never used to train public or proprietary machine learning models.

⚡

Deterministic Pipelines

All quantitative calculations (KPIs, margins, Pareto cuts, IQR anomalies) are executed with deterministic Python algorithms, guaranteeing full traceability to source data.

🔐

TLS 1.3 Transport Encryption

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is secured using modern TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HTTPS enforcement.

🗑️

Instant Session Purging

When you close your workspace tab or complete your export, session memory is cleared. Users maintain 100% control over their files.

📜

Reliable Architecture

Designed for secure, reliable enterprise data operations with automated dependency scanning and strict access barriers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, straightforward answers about data privacy, calculations, and integrations.

What data formats can I upload?

Metriva supports CSV and Excel (XLSX, XLS) files up to 500MB on Pro plans (20MB on Free). The engine handles numeric, timestamp, and categorical records automatically.

Does Metriva store my business data?

No. Metriva operates on a zero-retention in-memory architecture. Your records are loaded into volatile RAM for profiling and are destroyed when your session ends.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. We strictly never use customer files, transaction amounts, or business records to train public or proprietary machine learning models.

Can I connect Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Direct API connectors for Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, and Shopify are actively in development on our roadmap. Today, you can export and upload your CSV reports in seconds.

Do I need technical or SQL knowledge?

None at all. Metriva is designed for operators and business leaders. Dimension mapping, KPI calculations, and anomaly audits happen automatically.

How accurate are the insights?

Quantitative calculations (KPIs, margins, growth percentages, IQR outliers) use deterministic Python code that maps directly to your source data, while AI generates clear explanations.
