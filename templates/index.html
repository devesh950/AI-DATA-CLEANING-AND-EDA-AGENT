Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Have questions before trying? We're here to help.

Is my financial and client data secure?

↓
Yes, 100%. DataScope processes your uploaded spreadsheets ephemerally in server memory during your active session. We do not store your files on persistent disks, and we never use your proprietary numbers for model training.

What formats are supported?

↓
DataScope natively handles .CSV, .XLSX, and .XLS files. It automatically handles varied delimiter types, uncleaned headers, date formatting differences, and currency symbols.

How does the "Ask DataScope" feature work?

↓
You can type plain-English questions like "Who generated the most revenue in Q2?" or "What is our gross margin?" The built-in heuristic AI parses your question and computes the exact numbers, ranking, or margin leaks instantly.

Can I download the cleaned spreadsheet?

↓
Yes! With 1 click, DataScope cleans duplicate rows, fills missing values, standardizes date formats, and exports a pristine .CSV file ready for your ERP or accountant.

Can I deploy this to my own cloud infrastructure?

↓
Yes, the codebase is completely independent and includes standard configurations for Vercel, Render, Railway, AWS ECS, and Docker.
